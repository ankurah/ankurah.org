# Synchronization: an interactive guide

Follow one query's result from a node's local copy to a ready result: what the
requester offers, what the responder answers and pushes, and how the requester
repairs what does not arrive.

**[Start with a Fetch →](synchronization/index.html)**

The walkthrough shows one step at a time. Choose an empty, small or large local
copy, how much of it differs and whether an update is lost, then follow the path
those choices take. Exact messages and replies are available beneath each step.

After Fetch, continue with [what a live query adds](synchronization/livequery.html).
The [protocol reference](synchronization/reference.html) collects the
message types, tree vocabulary, readiness rules and limits.

Digest trees are held in memory; storage-backed trees and durable replication
are not implemented.
