# Synchronization: an interactive guide

Follow the lifecycle of one request, from local knowledge to an installed result.

**[Start with a Fetch →](../learn/synchronization/index.html)**

The walkthrough shows one step at a time. Choose an empty, small or large local
result, then follow the different comparison paths. Exact messages and replies
are available beneath each step.

After Fetch, continue with [what a live query adds](../learn/synchronization/livequery.html).
The [protocol reference](../learn/synchronization/reference.html) collects the
message types, tree vocabulary and lifetime rules.

Digest trees are held in memory; storage-backed trees and durable replication
are not implemented.
