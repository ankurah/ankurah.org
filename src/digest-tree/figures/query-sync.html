This is a conceptual sequence, not a recorded execution or a network measurement. The three spans are illustrative; the tree determines actual page bounds. A step can represent several bounded exchanges. Every disclosure remains subject to current authorization and work limits.

Matching digests finish comparison without requesting more pages or entity repairs. Otherwise, pages expose smaller span summaries or members; equal spans need no further descent. The requester reconciles differing members against its canonical entity heads, then receives bounded repairs over the peer's existing update channel. Query replies carry comparison control rather than a second entity delivery stream.

Coverage means that each target member is present locally at its captured head or a descendant. Concurrent heads need causal event repair. Local-only members are checked by authorized identity reads to learn about predicate exits; absence alone does not delete an entity. A lost retained version requires a new comparison, preserving repairs already applied and respecting the remaining work allowance.

For a live query, the peer installs its standing Reactor subscription before capturing the target. Comparison handles initialization or reconnect; Reactor continues to supply live changes.
